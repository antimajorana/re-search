Check a two-site equilibrium

Two sites are coupled by identical unit springs between fixed walls. A unit force acts on site 1. Their displacements satisfy \(2x-y=1\) and \(-x+2y=0\).

A proposed solution is \(x=2/3,\ y=-1/3\). Does it balance the forces?

Predict the outcome

Substitution check

The second equation gives \(y=x/2\). Substituting into the first gives \(3x/2=1\), hence \(x=2/3,\ y=1/3\).

Inspect the calculation

Keep both displacements in thirds. For numerators \(a,b\), the net force has numerators \(2a-b,-a+2b\). The target is \((3,0)\).
