## Inspect a research claim

- Choose **Trace a claim** beneath a saved answer and select a statement or equation. Follow cited sources, inspect matching proof entries and their recorded calculations, or request an independent review of the exact wording. The session keeps a record of the review.
- Select an assumption in **Proof DAG** to see which steps and conclusions depend on it, directly or through another step. **Check record integrity** checks the saved events and files.
- Open the worked example from the welcome screen to predict a result, inspect the computation, and diagnose a sign error without connecting a model.

## Recover your work

- Organize sessions into folders from the session list. Filter by folder while searching or viewing archived sessions. Removing a folder keeps its sessions.
- The manuscript editor offers to restore unsaved drafts after a restart. Restoring a draft lets you inspect it before saving to the workspace.
- Use **Projects → Back up project** and **Restore backup** to move a project with its sessions, folder assignments, research evidence, and private validation data. Keep your external manuscript workspace separately and reattach it after restore. Backups support up to 60 MiB compressed and 256 MiB expanded.

The Windows x64 installer includes the Python math runtime. The installer is unsigned. The release includes its SHA-256 checksum.
