## Following a research result

- Open **Canvas → Sources** to see the papers cited in the answer and the files linked to its proof. Follow a link to the relevant step or read a recorded passage. Search History keeps other results available.
- Scroll up during a live answer to read earlier work. Return to the bottom to follow new output.
- In-core models can name a session once they identify its topic. Equations render offline across the conversation, proof graph, tool results, and project views.
- The verifier receives recorded results from linked SymPy and expression checks. The workbench distinguishes a review of the full answer from individual step verdicts.
- Manuscript drafts stay available while switching files and sessions in the open app. If another program edits a file, the editor flags the conflict before saving.
- HTML, LaTeX, and ZIP research reports include completed answers, including turns without a proof graph. Reopening a CLI session also restores answers supplied only in its final result.

The Windows x64 installer includes the Python math runtime. Existing sessions and settings carry forward. The installer is unsigned. Its SHA-256 checksum is included with the release.
